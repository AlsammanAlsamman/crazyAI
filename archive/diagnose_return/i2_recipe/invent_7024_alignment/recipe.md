CHOSEN SEED: every crossing's fire-pile is stored in its own room, the failed and twice-broken ones sink into an underground tower, and only the highest pile is brought back up as the final count.

1. Lay the first cord flat along the near edge of the water table, in full sun, coil by coil from left to right, each coil cut to the exact shape of one symbol, touching nose to tail, and do not move this row again for the whole work.
2. Lay the second cord behind it, along the shadow-line, the same way — coil by coil, nose to tail, its first coil standing opposite the first coil of the near row.
3. Empty the tower below of every old room, and hang one empty room at the queen's threshold for the crossing you are about to walk. This first crossing is the straight one: no door may break in it.
4. Walk the crossing. Step once along both rows together, coil against coil. Where the near coil and the shadow coil are the same shape, the sun windows through both and one fire drops to the water — let it drop into this crossing's room. Where they refuse each other, drop nothing and step on anyway.
5. Keep stepping until one row runs out of coils. Then close the room, and let it hang at the threshold with whatever fires it holds.
6. Now count the quarrels you saw on the straight walk and mark each place where one happened: each such place is one attempt still owed.
7. For the first place still owed, hang a new empty room, and walk the crossing again from the very first coil, dropping fires exactly as in step 4 — until you reach that marked place. There, break the door once: let the shadow row's coil crouch forward past the quarrel, skip it, and hold every shadow coil after it one step out of step with the near row for the rest of the walk.
8. Finish that walk to the end of the shorter row, still dropping a fire wherever the two facing coils agree. If a second quarrel tempts you to break the door again, refuse — the door breaks once per crossing only. Close the room and hang it at the threshold.
9. Do steps 7 and 8 over again, once for every place still owed from step 6, each with its own fresh room, each breaking the door at its own single place and nowhere else.
10. Do the same the other way round: for each place still owed, hang another room and walk again, but this time let the near row's coil crouch forward past the quarrel, skipping the near coil instead of the shadow one, holding that offset to the end. Close each room at the threshold.
11. Any walk you spoil — a door broken twice, a row run past its end, a fire dropped where the shapes did not truly agree — cut its room loose and let it sink into the tower below, unread. It is no longer counted.
12. When no place is still owed, every hanging room is closed, and only the spoiled ones lie in the tower, go along the threshold and compare the piles by height. Bring up the single room whose pile of fire burned highest, and hand back that count of fires and nothing else — not the coils, not the broken door, not the rooms that lost. You are finished when there is no unwalked place left owed and one pile stands above all the rest.
