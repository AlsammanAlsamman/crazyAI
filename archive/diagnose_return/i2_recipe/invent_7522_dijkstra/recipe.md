CHOSEN SEED: Each round, the leader duck holding the shortest carried thread among the unstoned is found and settled before any other duck moves.

1. Lay out the grid of triangular houses so every corner is visible, and set beside each corner a bare thread spool. Write on the traveler's own corner a thread length of nothing; write on every other corner a thread so long no duck could ever carry it. Every corner starts unstoned.

2. Look over all unstoned corners that wear a thread short enough to be carried — that is, any thread but the impossible ones. If there are none left, go to step 10.

3. Walk the grid with your hand hovering above it and compare thread against thread, corner against corner, one at a time, holding in mind only the shortest you have yet touched. The corner still holding when your hand reaches the last unstoned corner is the leader duck's landing place.

4. Do not trust the leader on sight — it hides among its own long cousins. Trace its thread back shuttle by shuttle, corner by corner, to the traveler's own corner, adding the centimetres of each road as you go. If the traced sum does not match the written length, correct the written length and return to step 2. If it matches, the leader is true.

5. Place a black stone on that corner with a dark, artery click. Its thread length is now final and is never touched, compared, or rewritten again for as long as the work lasts.

6. From the newly stoned corner, take each road leading out, one road at a time, in whatever order they lie.

7. For the road in hand, add its centimetres to the stoned corner's final length. That sum is the fresh duck's thread.

8. Compare the fresh thread against the thread already worn by the corner at the road's far side. If the corner is already stoned, throw the fresh thread away unspooled. If the fresh thread is shorter, cut away the old thread, write the fresh length on that corner, and note beside it that the stoned corner is the shuttle it came through. If the fresh thread is the same length or longer, throw it away unspooled. Take the next road and repeat from step 7 until no roads remain out of this stone.

9. Return to step 2 and do the whole round again: find the leader, trace it, stone it, loose fresh ducks along its roads.

10. You are finished when no unstoned corner wears a carryable thread. Every corner bearing a black stone holds its true shortest length and its shuttle, so the road back can be read corner by corner. Every corner still bare, still wearing the impossible thread, no road ever reached — leave it bare; its light is simply lost.
