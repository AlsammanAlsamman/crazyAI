Stripping away the setting, what's left is a layered structure of **repurposed instrumentation** — not one clean relation but three or four stacked ones, and I think it's more honest to name them separately than to flatten them into a single formula.

**1. An expressive artifact grafted onto an existing mechanical process.**
Something is built to *hold* or *voice* an unresolved quantity (grief, in this case — but the slot could hold anything unbearable to leave silent) by attaching it to machinery that was already moving for an unrelated reason (haulage). The artifact doesn't generate its own motion; it rides a pre-existing force. This is the general move: *take a process already running, and let it drive a second, symbolic output as a side effect.*

**2. A derivative standing in for a hidden variable.**
The output (bell-speed) isn't a direct reading of the thing anyone actually cares about (contested/dangerous water, "ghosts' claim"). It's the *rate of strain in the coupling mechanism* — itself a downstream effect of that hidden thing. So the chain is:

    hidden condition → resistance/strain on a physical linkage → rate of a symbolic output → community's read of that rate

Nobody measures the hidden condition. Everyone has learned to decode a proxy of a proxy, calibrated only by convention ("slow = calm," "fast/stuttering = claimed"), with no verification loop back to ground truth.

**3. Retroactive functionalization.**
The device was not built to inform anyone. Meaning was assigned *after* the fact, socially, by watching correlations accumulate — the instrument's use precedes its interpretation, and its interpretation precedes any design intent. This is the inverse of engineering: function discovered by a population living alongside a thing, not specified by a maker.

**4. A ritual response with its own independent hazard, chained to the reading.**
The signal doesn't just inform — it triggers a prescribed counter-action (hang a lily), and that action carries a *second, unrelated risk* (dropped wrong, it injures someone), which is most dangerous exactly when the primary signal is most urgent (stuttering days). So risk compounds at the moment of highest alert rather than being mitigated by it — the safety behavior and the danger it responds to are correlated in when they're most fragile.

I don't think this reduces to one equation or symmetry — it's closer to a *pipeline of substitutions* (grief → mechanism → rate → social meaning → ritual → secondary risk), each link opportunistic rather than designed, and the whole thing only holds together because a population kept re-anchoring meaning onto whatever the previous link happened to produce. The interesting part isn't any single mapping in that chain — it's that none of the links were chosen for their downstream role, yet the chain functions anyway.
