**The shape, stripped of scenery:**

A bounded, self-metered descent, gated by an audible proxy-signal rather than by the underlying quantity itself, with asymmetric failure under desynchronization, and a companion process that begins in a silent verifying stance and only converts to active parallel participation once a specific internal condition (loss of self-referential grip) is met.

Unpacked into its separable parts:

1. **Bounded draw, units by feel.** A single grasp from a source that caps itself ("never more than fills one hand") is converted into a discrete count — determined by internal sensation, not external measurement. *Quantity → tally.*

2. **Reoriented reference frame.** The relevant "down" is redefined away from the literal ground toward a chosen authority overhead. The whole mechanism runs on a substituted gravity — one that must be watched, not merely obeyed.

3. **Signal-gated transition, not magnitude-gated.** The gate (boulder) does not read the resource or the tally directly — it reads a rhythmic acoustic emission (wood-on-wood) produced *from* the tally, one strike per unit, and releases one increment of motion per strike. Trust is placed in the *sound of counting*, not in the count itself.

4. **Asymmetric synchrony constraint.** Two processes must stay locked — the emitting of signal and the change of state. Outrunning the signal (falling ahead of clicking) preserves the outer/global condition (calm sea) but costs the integrity of the one moving ("not whole"). Lagging behind it (clicking without falling) costs nothing but time — the gate simply idles. Same desynchrony, opposite direction, different order of consequence: one is a quiet cost paid outwardly, the other a real cost paid inwardly, and gate stalls in the second case rather than punishing.

5. **A witness keyed to process-honesty, not outcome.** A second party is present throughout, attending only to whether the tally-generating hand is truthful — indifferent to the eventual result the gate will produce.

6. **Threshold-triggered mode change in the witness.** The witness converts from passive verification to active identical co-participation, but only once a specific interior condition fires: the counter no longer needs to hold on to confirm its own identity. That single event — cessation of self-grasping — is what licenses the shift from solitary metered process to shared, unmetered company.

**Where it resists clean reduction:** points 1–4 form something close to a formal control-loop or rate-limiter (bounded sample → discrete signal → gated state transition → synchrony constraint with direction-dependent failure modes) — that part compresses well. But points 5–6 introduce a second agent whose activation condition is not a threshold *on the same variable being counted* but on an unrelated interior state (self-referential grip releasing) of the counting agent. That's a cross-domain trigger — a control system whose companion-mode switch is keyed to something the control system itself has no formal access to. I can't fold that into the same clean relation without either trivializing it (just calling it "a second condition") or over-claiming a mechanism I don't actually see stated. So: mostly one structure (metered, trust-gated, synchrony-constrained release), plus one real seam where a psychological/identity threshold does work that the rest of the formalism can't account for on its own terms.
