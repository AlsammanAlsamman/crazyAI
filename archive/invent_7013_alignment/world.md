# World (blend model: markov, imagination score 0.5346)

An owl-headed woman sits at a door that opens onto another door and another, an endless corridor of doors. A green-faced man and a single cracked pillar can bring the roof down on everyone inside. Each stroke she paints becomes a living bird, feathers still wet, lifting off the paper into the watery hush of the source, becoming pure melody that isn't his own heart, never certain which guest is finally arriving. No rung is higher, every twig alive today is exactly the visitor's own, seen at the mouth of a lemur. Everything in this ballad is stained green — the wind, the mind must already hold the ending and the trunk is not a destination but a crowd of dead ancestors holding the living up.
