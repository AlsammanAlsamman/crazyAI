I go to the wells first, for each heap is a well and its depth is counted by objects lying loose in it. I draw up not the objects but a thread from the kite spool, and fold it in half against the tube, and half again, and half again, until the tube can take no more folds, marking each fold with a knot of wax if a whole segment remains unmatched, and blank if it halves clean — this ladder of knots and blanks I lay across the wells' rim, one ladder per well. Then I take the lens the servants use for reading omens and hold it over all the ladders stacked together, rung against rung, and wherever a rung shows an odd number of waxed knots among the wells, that rung is restless and will not sit still until answered. If every rung under the lens shows even knots, I let the round pass to whoever moves next and wait, calm as the kite sky, for their disturbance to make a rung restless again. But if a rung is restless, I find the highest one, choose any well whose ladder shows a knot there, and unwind that well's thread entirely off the tube, rewinding only as much as makes every restless rung on its ladder flip and match the others — the rest I throw into the foam and it is gone from the count for good. I do this each turn the restlessness allows, and by the wells emptying under this ritual, the last object taken from the last well is always mine.

SEED: Each well's depth is converted to a fold-ladder of waxed knots and blanks by repeated halving against the tube.
SEED: All ladders are stacked and read through the omen-lens together, and any rung with an odd count of knots across the wells is marked restless.
SEED: The highest restless rung is answered by unwinding one well's thread and rewinding it to flip that rung and all below into agreement, discarding the excess into the foam.
