I take the message as a line of cups, each cup holding one small token of story, and I walk it into the wider aboard of dark where corridors stand from every original at once. Each corridor is numbered by how the nightingales knock at Fuji's shut door — once, twice, in fours, in eights — and I send each cup down every corridor whose number is folded into its own position in the line, the way a week folds into six days and a seventh that isn't quite the same. A cup can walk more than one corridor; that is allowed, that is the whole trick. At the far end of each corridor stands a single closed door, and whatever cups arrive there I pour together into one basin, story into story, by the strange arithmetic that governs this house: pour a story into itself and the basin comes up empty, pour it in twice more and it's as if it never came, only an odd arrival leaves a mark in the wax. I do not add, I do not count — I only pour and watch what refuses to vanish. What's left in each basin, after every cup has finished its walk, I skim off as a new small token — not a copy of any story, but the residue of a corridor, the shape amber takes when everything else has boiled away. I wait for the basins to go still, for the wave in each one to lie flat and glassy, because a basin still stirring hasn't finished remembering what passed through it. Once still, I lift the residues out one by one, in the same order their corridors stood, and string them after the last true cup in the line — that trailing string is the seal, short because there are far fewer doors than there are cups, and worth as much as its shortness lets it be. Everything else — the corridor-map scratched into the tavern wall, the count of which cup went where — I let the sky have; I don't carry it forward, because whoever must mend a damaged cup later works backward from the seal alone: pours the surviving cups of a corridor back into its basin, and whatever mark refuses to vanish this time points straight at the empty cup and, because the arithmetic erases doubles, at what story it must have carried. Then Fuji is knocked at again, precisely, and stays unvisited, the way it always does when the work is done right.
