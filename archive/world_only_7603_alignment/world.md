Sun, moon, earth, and every living creature strung on it like beads. Ivy has grown through the library shelves, and owls nest in rooms whose doors were sealed a hundred years before anyone now living was born. A railway station at night, lit by cold lamps. Women in long dresses stand motionless on the platform while a skeleton sits on a bench reading. Chalk lines scratched across pavement into a ladder of numbered squares, a corridor swept — and no one alive remembers why, only that the Earl himself is bound to perform them or the stones themselves would somehow know.
