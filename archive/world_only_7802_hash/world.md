# World (blend model: markov, imagination score 0.3118)

Learning the language rearranges the linguist's sense of time until past and future feel equally present, equally fixed. The perspective is wrong in a way that feels correct — lines converge toward a vanishing point that never arrives. A brick tower with a clock frozen at no legible hour rises over an empty horizon for a rider who will never come back. When one organ sickens the others compensate until they cannot. She names her grief a fire lit inside the ribs that no nearby well could ever drown, and everyone stands on the station copies of the ground itself.
